The City - Resources and Opportunities

A fictional saga set in the year 9925 — stories from The City of Paras.

It was a day after meeting professor Adin, Reev woke up and got to the washroom in a blink of an eye. In the background there was a voice telling me, “Do you want me to turn off the upcoming alarm?” Yes go ahead and do that, said Reev.

Walking out in the streets, there were not so many people walking in the street. There were two already sitting in the pod as he entered into one. After a minute there was a background voice telling him, please select the destination to start. But he didn’t react, after a distractive sound, it again repeated. Suddenly Reev startled and talked out loud Agro Farm Unit 587. The doors closed and the pod started moving.

“Did you say you are heading to Agro Farm Unit 587?” the old man had spoken up. Pivoting slowly Reev questioned “Are you speaking to me?”

“Yes you were shouting the name Agro Farm Unit 587. I am travelling to that place, so I was just wondering if you are also heading there” the man answered.

“Oh that, sorry for that, I was trying to communicate with Jeev. But, yes I am working there as a research scientist. Do you also work there? I couldn’t remember seeing you there” replied Reev while looking at the light trails through the glasses of the pod. He can’t see anything more than that. Meanwhile he was still thinking about the previous day’s conversation with the professor Adin.

He was wondering about what the professor might decide about the project? Would he accept the proposal? Also he remembered that the professor told him that he is not the only person to make a decision. He will discuss the proposal with the research committee about this and based on their voting only everything will be decided. He also remembered that he very much liked Reev’s different approach to this problem and will advocate for this at his best.

“Hello, are you listening to me? It seems like you are thinking about something” The old man asked

“Sorry I got distracted by my thoughts. What were you saying?”

“I was saying that I am not an employee there and just going to meet someone” the old man replied. The old man thought he was not interested in the conversation and went silent for a few minutes. He was looking outside the stations that passed by for a few minutes. Finally they reached the Agro Farm station. Both of them got down from the pod and started walking towards the entrance of the facility.

The old man scanned his palm and the system asked him to wait in a common area for 30 mins. He was looking around the place and sat on one of the chairs placed there. There were people coming and going. After a few minutes Reev came and sat on a nearby chair and again the meeting with the professor came to his mind. He wanted to stop that, so he started a conversation with the old man this time.

“Hello sir, can I ask who are you waiting to meet?” Asked Reev with an undertone.

“I came here to meet my daughter, you might know her, she is also a research scientist here, her name is Maia”

“Oh you are Maia’s father, I am Reev and both of us work on the same project.” Reev shouted. There was a sudden smile on his face, but his heartbeat raised. “It’s nice to meet you sir, she didn’t tell me about you coming to meet her” he stuttered.

“She didn’t know about it until yesterday, I came here for work and thought of meeting her,” the old man explained.

“She had told me about you working on some committee for the country’s infrastructure and living standard development, what do you guys do about it?” Reev questioned him again. He nudged into the edge of the seat and slightly leaned forward. His eyes were wide open now and he totally forgot about the things bothering him from the morning.

“Oh that’s a long story, kid, this journey started when I was just 15. At that time there were not many good learning centres, so I luckily got into the good one. I started to work as an assistant in the planning committee for some extra credits. But later realised I really liked that and pursued the same for a long time now”. Meanwhile more people started to arrive at the facility and some were leaving the facility. Reev didn’t seem to care about anyone and he was fully engaged in this conversation.

“What did the committee formed for?” Reev started inquiring.

“A few decades ago the world didn’t look like what it is currently? There were a lot of differences in economy and life standards in different parts of the country. It had accumulated over the long course of history.” said the old man.

“Not trying to be arrogant but still I can see those differences sir” Reev whispered.

“I get your point kid, you see people tend to naturally relocate themselves where there are more opportunities and resources. So that effect will still be there, but you are barely seeing the tip of the iceberg now, this difference used to be like an ant and an elephant.” the old man countered.

There was a background voice reminding Reev that shift starts in 15 mins. Maia was entering the facility as they were immersed in their conversation. Both of them missed noticing her, but Maia spotted both of them and distracted their conversation by greeting her father first and looked into Reev with surprise.

“Did you know my father?” Maia gasped.

“No no, we just met in the pod and later came to know that he is your father, nothing to worry” he teased her.

“Wait a minute Maia, we were in the middle of something, just let us finish it and then you can take your father with you” he continued.

“We were just talking about my work Maia, So kid listen to this, people who lived in the remote or sub urban places got less and less access to the resources and opportunities. The people who lived in an overly populated area were becoming overcrowded and hard to manage.” explained the old man. Maia gasped and sat down on the chair with a slight smile on her face.

“we restructured the whole country into 10 states, each state with 10 or more cities. Each city was categorised as tier-1, tier-2 and tier-3 based on their current status. This tier of the city was decided based on goods production, population density and people’s living standards. Our plan was to balance these differences in such a way that it benefits both sets of people” the old man continued.

“Dad, I know that you will never stop explaining and Reev also won’t stop asking questions about whatever you tell him. So you guys should continue this on another day. Reev should go to work now and I only got permission for 30 mins” said Maia.

All of them were laughing, Maia and her father strolled towards the cafetière. Reev also hurried for work. While leaving Reev started wondering if people are so much focused on opportunities and resources, what would happen if people didn’t have enough resources to satisfy all of them.
