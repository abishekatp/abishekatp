---
title: The Weather - Life is Like the Weather
date: 2026-01-16
description: No, this is not about being unpredictable or uncontrollable.
---

This morning, I woke up and noticed that it was raining. It was not like the rain during the monsoon, which would make me worry about my commute to the office. There was something different about it. When I started to commute to work, the rain had already stopped, but the weather didn't change back to normal. It didn't feel like a regular summar in Chennai. It felt pleasant and refreshing.

Then the whole day felt like that. The same places that I walked around the office felt a little bit different today. Everyone seemed a little bit happier than usual. In fact, someone asked me why I looked more happy today. At that time, I didn't know the answer to that question. But later when I thought about it, the sudden rain in the middle of a hot summer was the reason for that extra smile on my face.

Then I checked on the internet if the weather and environment actually have this much power. It says there is proven research suggesting that weather, sunlight, and the environment can greatly influence our mood, energy, and cognition.

When we are at a mild or moderate temperature around 20 to 22 degrees Celsius, it tends to boost our mood and happiness, while excessive heat influences our bad mood. But this absolute number does not work for everyone. This is the global average and greatly changes based on your location, lifestyle, age, and many other factors. Also, sunlight triggers the brain to release serotonin, which regulates happiness and calmness, while a dark environment triggers the brain to release melatonin, which regulates sleep.

But did the cold weather actually improve my mood here? No, here the science is different — our brain releases dopamine when it experiences something new in the middle of monotonous days.

Science apart, this reminded me of something else. In our life also, there will be rainy days in the middle of a very hot summer, or sunny days in the middle of a very hectic monsoon. Both of them make us happy. You see, we only appreciate it when we get it as a break, but don't appreciate it enough when we get something abundantly.

I hope there will always be some rainy days in the middle of the summer to remind me of this.
